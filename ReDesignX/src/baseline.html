Summit Mission

A Pragmatic Forum for Decarbonization

Apex Horizon Summit was founded on the conviction that reaching global net-zero targets requires pragmatic engineering, capital mobilization, and coordinated public policy. We bring together key decision-makers across renewable energy, grid modernization, industrial decarbonization, and nature-based solutions.

Our 2027 agenda focuses on real-world deployment challenges: overcoming transmission interconnection bottlenecks, scaling long-duration energy storage, certifying carbon removal integrity, and financing critical infrastructure in emerging markets.

Venue & Attendee Logistics

Apex Horizon Summit 2027 takes place at the Moscone Convention Center in downtown San Francisco, California. The venue is fully accessible, certified LEED Platinum, and powered entirely by renewable electricity.

  • Location: Moscone Convention Center, 747 Howard Street, San Francisco, CA 94103
  • Public Transit: Two blocks from Powell Street BART and Muni Metro Station
  • Accessibility: Full wheelchair accessibility, assistive listening systems, and live captioning available
  • Virtual Option: High-definition livestreaming and interactive Q&A available for virtual ticket holders
