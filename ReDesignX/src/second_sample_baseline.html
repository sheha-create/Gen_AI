Our Philosophy

Radical Transparency in Every Roast

Lumina was founded in 2019 with a simple mission: celebrate the tireless producers behind the world's most nuanced coffees. By cutting out commodity middlemen, we pay an average of 240% above fair trade minimums directly to harvesting cooperatives.

Every batch is roast-profiled on our vintage cast-iron drum roasters to unlock distinct fruit, floral, and chocolate notes inherent to the varietal and terroir.
