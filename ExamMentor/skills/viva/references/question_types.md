# ExamMentor Viva Question Blueprint & Cognitive Types

To ensure a rigorous and balanced oral exam, ExamMentor constructs question plans spanning 5 cognitive categories, all grounded directly in the provided notes.

---

## The 5 Cognitive Viva Question Categories

### 1. Definition Questions (Recall & Precision)
- **Objective**: Test the learner's ability to recall and precisely define foundational terminology, metrics, states, or concepts.
- **Stem Patterns**:
  - *"According to your notes, how is [Concept] formally defined?"*
  - *"What are the primary characteristics that define [Term]?"*
- **Evaluation Criteria**: Checks for presence of essential keywords, boundary definitions, and formal terminology.

### 2. Explanation Questions (Mechanisms & Process Flow)
- **Objective**: Assess the learner's understanding of how a mechanism works, step-by-step lifecycles, or underlying logic.
- **Stem Patterns**:
  - *"Explain the step-by-step process of [Mechanism/Procedure] as outlined in the text."*
  - *"How does [System Component] transition from State A to State B during [Event]?"*
- **Evaluation Criteria**: Checks for sequence accuracy, causal links, and completeness of stages.

### 3. Comparison Questions (Contrast & Discrimination)
- **Objective**: Test the learner's ability to distinguish between two related concepts, architectures, strategies, or modes.
- **Stem Patterns**:
  - *"What is the primary operational distinction between [Concept A] and [Concept B]?"*
  - *"Compare [Method X] and [Method Y] in terms of performance and overhead as described in your notes."*
- **Evaluation Criteria**: Validates that both sides of the comparison are accurately characterized, highlighting key differentiators.

### 4. Application Questions (Scenario & Problem Solving)
- **Objective**: Present a concrete situational context or configuration and ask the learner to apply the notes' principles to solve it.
- **Stem Patterns**:
  - *"Suppose a system experiences [Specific Symptom]. Based on your notes, what is the root cause and what action should be taken?"*
  - *"Given the code/configuration snippet in your notes, what happens if [Parameter] is set to [Value]?"*
- **Evaluation Criteria**: Assesses contextual mapping, application of rule/formula, and practical validity.

### 5. Reasoning Questions (Trade-offs & Rationale)
- **Objective**: Evaluate higher-order critical thinking—why an architecture was chosen, trade-offs involved, or failure implications.
- **Stem Patterns**:
  - *"Why does [Protocol/Algorithm] enforce [Constraint]? What failure occurs if this constraint is violated?"*
  - *"What are the key trade-offs between [Design A] and [Design B] according to your material?"*
- **Evaluation Criteria**: Checks logical cause-and-effect reasoning grounded strictly in the material's stated trade-offs.
