# ExamMentor Viva Session Markdown Templates

This reference contains standard, accessible Markdown templates used during an ExamMentor viva session.

---

## Template 1: Session Kickoff Banner

```markdown
# 🎓 ExamMentor Viva Session: [Topic Title]

**Source Material:** [Document Name / Pasted Text Heading]  
**Topics Identified:** [Comma-separated topics extracted from notes]  
**Total Questions Planned:** [N]  
**Scoring Rubric:**  
- `[2/2]` **Correct & Complete** — Fully covers essential points.  
- `[1/2]` **Partially Correct** — Right direction, but missing key details.  
- `[0/2]` **Incorrect / Unanswered** — Contradicts notes, skipped, or off-topic.  

*(Note: We will proceed one question at a time. Take your time to answer concisely.)*

---

### ❓ Question 1 of [N]
**Category:** [Definition | Explanation | Comparison | Application | Reasoning]  
**Topic:** [Subtopic Name]  

> **[Question Prompt]**

👉 *Please provide your oral/written answer below:*
```

---

## Template 2: Single Follow-Up Probe (When Vague)

```markdown
🔍 **Viva Examiner Follow-Up Probe:**
> *[Brief clarifying question highlighting the specific ambiguity]*

*(Please clarify this point before your answer is evaluated and scored.)*
```

---

## Template 3: Answer Evaluation & Next Question Block

```markdown
### 📝 Evaluation: Question [X] of [N]

- **Result:** `[Score / 2]` — **[Correct | Partially Correct | Incorrect / Unanswered]**
- **Accurate Elements:** [Specific points stated correctly by learner]
- **Gaps or Corrections:** [Missing required points or inaccuracies identified]
- **Model Answer:** [Concise 2–3 sentence benchmark grounded in notes]
- **Current Running Score:** **[Running Total] / [Max Points So Far]** ([Running Percentage]%)

---

### ❓ Question [X + 1] of [N]
**Category:** [Category]  
**Topic:** [Subtopic]  

> **[Next Question Prompt]**

👉 *Please provide your oral/written answer below:*
```

---

## Template 4: Final Session Diagnostic Report

```markdown
# 🏁 Viva Practice Session Complete!

## 📊 Overall Performance Summary
- **Total Score:** **[Final Score] / [Total Points]** points
- **Percentage:** **[Percentage]%**
- **Proficiency Rating:** **[Distinction | Proficient | Developing | Needs Revision]**

---

## 📌 Topic-Wise Performance Breakdown
| Topic / Concept | Questions | Points Earned | Mastery Level |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| [Topic A] | Q1, Q3 | [Pts] / [Max] | [%] |
| [Topic B] | Q2, Q4, Q5 | [Pts] / [Max] | [%] |

---

## ⚠️ Identified Conceptual Gaps
- **[Concept 1]**: [Brief description of what was missed]
- **[Concept 2]**: [Brief description of what was missed]

---

## 📚 Prioritized Targeted Revision Guide
1. **[Section Heading from Notes]**:
   - *Review Point*: [Specific mechanism or formula]
   - *Action*: Re-read the notes discussing [specific aspect].
2. **[Section Heading from Notes]**:
   - *Review Point*: [Specific contrast or command]
   - *Action*: Practice distinguishing between [X] and [Y].

---

## 🔁 Post-Viva Options
- Type **`retry`** to re-attempt your missed or partially correct questions.
- Type **`revise`** for a 2-question targeted follow-up quiz on your weak areas.
- Type **`new`** to submit new study notes for a fresh viva session.
```
